The funniest final text a dying phone sends at one percent battery — written in the phone's own voice, under 200 characters.

> dying at 1%. the charger was in the kitchen drawer the whole time. i watched you tear the house apart for three days. worth it. goodbye.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (136 characters, one line, inside the 1–200 gate).
